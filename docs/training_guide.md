# BuildCast: training guide for estimators

**Time needed:** about 20 minutes. **You will learn:** how to get a forecast for a new job, how to read the range,
and when to trust it.

## 1. What BuildCast is for

BuildCast gives you a second opinion on labour and materials before you price a job. It learns from the
company's finished jobs: the hours actually booked on timesheets and the materials actually bought. It does not
replace your judgement. It shows you what the company's own history says.

## 2. Getting a forecast (5 minutes)

1. Open the app and stay on **Estimate a new job**.
2. Fill in what you know at tender stage: sector, region, type of work, specification, floor area, floors,
   scope, sprinklers, whether the site is occupied, the offsite prefabrication share, the planned programme and the
   start date.
3. Press **Estimate**.

You get total labour hours and cost, then a line for each trade and material.

## 3. Reading the range

Each line shows a **prediction** and a **likely range (90%)**. On jobs it had never seen, the actual figure landed
inside the range about 9 times in 10.

- A narrow range means past jobs like this one were consistent.
- A wide range means they varied a lot, so allow more contingency or look at the drawings more closely.
- If your own estimate is outside the range, it is worth a second look. It may be right (you know something the
  history doesn't), but it should be a decision, not an accident.

## 4. Checking the "similar past jobs"

Under the forecast are the five most similar finished jobs, with their actual hours per m². This is the evidence
behind the number. Ask: are these really comparable? If not, note what's different.

## 5. When not to rely on it

- Job types the company has rarely done (the similar-jobs table will look thin).
- Very large or very small jobs compared with the history.
- Anything unusual the form doesn't capture: a difficult client, a tight site, a novel system.

## 6. Keeping the data good

The forecasts are only as good as the records behind them:

- Book timesheet hours to the right job code.
- If the **Data quality** tab shows unmatched jobs or a review queue, a few minutes' review fixes them for good.
- New purchase-order wording appears in the review queue once; mapping it once fixes every future order.
